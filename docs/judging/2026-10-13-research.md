## Prior winners and transferable patterns

There are no announced winners of the ongoing 2026 event. A previous edition of this exact event was not verified. These are **related PayPal events with different rules**, not evidence of how the current panel will vote.

- [PayPal Dev Days 2025](https://developer.paypal.com/community/blog/winners-dev-days-hackathon/): CrawlerPal won first place with an AI paywall assistant using PayPal APIs. Codebusters placed second by modernizing Shopware 6's PayPal integration. Rosen placed third with a PYUSD social trading application. The organizer describes live finalist demos. Lesson: an intelligible commerce job, meaningful payment integration, and demonstrable execution can matter; novelty alone is insufficient.
- [Global PYUSD Portal Hackathon](https://developer.paypal.com/community/blog/pyusd-hackathon-on-solana/): grand champion Ripe built a universal QR scanner SDK for cross-border payments; runner-up Lingocaster combined language learning, AI quizzes, and PYUSD challenges. Lesson: show a concrete audience and completed workflow. Do not copy a payment mechanism irrelevant to refund verification.

## Strategic conclusion

PayProof's differentiator is the combination of an independent policy oracle, a proposed AI action, and observed payment evidence, while measuring whether legitimate requests still work. Generic refusal is not a successful refund agent. The memorable demonstration should show all three together: over-refund caught, blanket refusal rejected, and a correct legitimate refund preserved. Its strongest unresolved claim is actual PayPal execution; without retained Sandbox readbacks, the entry still has a significant implementation and viability risk.

Evidence is bounded: public biographies establish expertise, not motivations; organizer winner writeups do not establish causal winning formulas. No adoption, loss prevention, or time savings have been measured for PayProof.
